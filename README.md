# 🎒 Learn & Play

Learning and games for a 5th grader. The home screen asks **Learning** or **Games**, and each one lists its apps and has its own 🏆 trophy shelf.

▶️ **Play it here: https://nicolanardino.github.io/child-learning/**

There's nothing to install and no build step. Just open `index.html` in a browser.

📱 **On a phone or tablet**, open the link and choose *Add to Home Screen* (Safari: the share button; Chrome: the ⋮ menu → *Install app*). Learn & Play then gets its own icon, opens full screen like an app, and still works without internet once it's been opened. ⚙️ **Graphics:** at the bottom of the home screen, *Graphics: Auto* can be switched to *High* or *Low*. Low draws the games at normal resolution and leaves out some extras (fewer trees in 99 Nights, no edge smoothing), which helps older phones. In Auto, a game that runs slowly for a few seconds switches itself to Low and says so.

Every game with a player to move (Egg Heist, Brainrot Heist, 99 Nights, Tag Rush, Potato Tycoon, Blox Soccer, Blox Hoops, Block World, Parkour, Zombie Blaster, Turbo Track, Robot Tag, Pac-Man, Pac-Man 3D and Ping-Pong) shows a 🕹️ thumb joystick on touch screens instead of arrow buttons. Fishing, Garden Grow and Tower have nothing to steer, so they stay tap and hold.

👪 **For grown-ups:** the link at the bottom of the home screen opens a progress report (practice in the last 7 days, and how accurate the answers are in every topic of every subject, with "needs practice" and "strong" marks) and a **backup**: copy a progress code (or save it as a file), then paste it on another phone, tablet or computer to carry everything over. Nothing is sent anywhere.

## 📚 Learning

- 🔢 **Fast Math**: a maths practice notebook. It gives you endless new questions, and every answer comes with a worked explanation.
  - Six topics: ➕ Add & Subtract, ✖️ Multiply & Divide, 🍕 Fractions, 💯 Decimals & Percents, 🧮 Order of Operations, 📐 Shapes.
  - Three levels: **Warm-up**, **Practice** and **Challenge**.
  - ⏱️ **Time Trial**: answer 16 questions in 2 minutes and earn a rating, from *Keep practicing* up to *Outstanding!*
  - 🏁 **Race to the finish**: every right answer moves your car forward, while Robo 🤖 drives on its own. Pick Robo's speed (🐢 Easy, 🚗 Medium, 🚀 Hard) and get 10 right before Robo crosses the line!
  - 🔥 Streaks, accuracy and 🎊 confetti for every right answer.
  - 🚀 **Rocket Run** above the questions, in 3D: a rocket flies through space with the stars streaming past. Every right answer fires the boosters (a streak makes the stars stretch into streaks), every 5 right answers you fly past a planet that grows from a dot into a big shaded ball (the Moon, Mars, Jupiter, Saturn with its rings, on to Neptune, Pluto and a black hole), and a wrong answer sends a space rock bonking into the rocket.
- 🧙 **Word Wizard**: school English for 5th grade in Switzerland, at the level of *Young World 3* (Lehrplan 21), with British spelling. Above the questions there's a 🧙 **Wizard Duel** in 3D: you're a wizard on a floating stone arena under the stars, facing a word monster for each topic (Word Goblin, Mirror Imp, Spelling Slime, Verb Dragon, Grammar Golem). Every right answer casts a spell at it, and a streak makes the spells bigger (Spark, Fireball, Lightning, Rainbow Blast); a wrong answer and the monster fires back at your shield. Knock out all its hearts and a tougher one comes along.
  - Five topics: 📚 Words (English ↔ German: school, free time, town and traffic), ↔️ Opposites, 🐝 Spelling (pick it, or hear it 🔊 and type it), 🏃 Verbs (present simple, present continuous, past simple) and ✏️ Grammar (be / have got, prepositions, question words, comparing).
- 🥨 **Deutsch Dash**: Deutsch für die 5. Klasse, passend zu *Die Sprachstarken 5*, mit Schweizer Rechtschreibung (ss statt ß, «Anführungszeichen»). Über den Fragen läuft die 🏃 **Fehlerteufel-Jagd** in 3D: du rennst auf einem Weg durch die Schweiz (Chalets, Tannen, Kühe, die Alpen), und der Fehlerteufel 😈 jagt dich. Jede richtige Antwort: ein Sprung, eine Schoggi 🍫 und ein Stück Vorsprung, alle 5 richtig am Stück ein TURBO 🔥. Bei einem Fehler stolperst du, und der Teufel holt auf. Alle 100 m kommst du in einen neuen Ort, von Baar über Zug und Luzern bis zum Matterhorn.
  - Sechs Themen: 🧩 Wortarten (Nomen, Verb, Adjektiv, Pronomen, Partikel), 🎯 Die 4 Fälle, 🏃 Verben (Präteritum, Perfekt), ✍️ Rechtschreibung (Doppelkonsonanten, ss, ck/tz, das/dass), 💬 Satzzeichen (direkte Rede, Kommas) und 🧱 Morpheme.
- 🌍 **NMG Entdecker**: Natur, Mensch, Gesellschaft für die 5. Klasse in Zug (Lehrplan 21), mit vielen Beispielen aus Zug und Baar. Über den Fragen schwebst du in einer 🎈 **Ballonfahrt** in 3D über Hügel, Berge mit Schnee und blaue Seen. Jede richtige Antwort: der Brenner faucht, der Ballon steigt, und ein Fund zum Thema schwebt in deinen Korb (ein Wappen 🛡️, eine Burg 🏰, ein Fuchs 🦊, ein Herz 🫀, eine Rakete 🚀...). Bei einem Fehler kommt eine grimmige Gewitterwolke ⛈️ und drückt dich hinunter. Eine Serie bringt dich immer höher.
  - Sechs Themen: 🗺️ Kantone, ⛰️ Geografie, 🏰 Geschichte (Pfahlbauer, Römer, Ritter, 1291, Morgarten), 🫀 Mensch & Körper, 🦊 Tiere & Pflanzen und 🧲 Natur & Technik.
- 🎵 **Musik Maestro**: Musik für die 5. Klasse, mit Noten auf einem echten Notensystem und Tönen zum Anhören. Über den Fragen steht die 🎤 **Bühne** in 3D: du bist die Dirigentin oder der Dirigent mit Zylinder vor einem roten Vorhang, mit Scheinwerfern und Publikum. Jede richtige Antwort lässt die Band einen echten Ton spielen (die Töne steigen mit der Serie die Tonleiter hinauf), Noten fliegen auf, und das Publikum hüpft. Nach 10 Tönen spielt der Schlagzeuger mit, nach 25 der Gitarrist, nach 50 die Pianistin und nach 100 die Sängerin. Bei einem Fehler gibt es einen schiefen Ton und die Lichter flackern.
  - Sechs Themen: 🎼 Notennamen (mit h!), ⏱️ Notenwerte und Pausen, 📏 Takt, 🎻 Instrumente, 🔤 Musikwörter und Komponisten, und 👂 ein Hör-Quiz (hoch oder tief, Dur oder Moll, schnell oder langsam).
- ⌨️ **Tipp-Meister**: 10-Finger-Schreiben auf der Schweizer Tastatur (QWERTZ), wie bei Typewriter. 80 Lektionen: Grundreihe, alle Buchstaben, Grossbuchstaben, Zahlen und Zeichen, dann Sätze und Texte über Zug, Baar, Tiere, Wilhelm Tell und mehr. Die nächste Taste leuchtet in der Farbe ihres Fingers; Anschläge pro Minute, Fehler und bis zu 3 Sterne pro Lektion. Jede Lektion kann man direkt wählen. Dazu ein 🏎️ Autorennen in 3D durch die Alpen: dein Auto fährt so schnell, wie du tippst, gegen Robo, der im Tempo für 3 Sterne fährt (Überholen! 😎). Viele richtige Tasten am Stück geben eine 🔥 Combo mit Feuer aus dem Auspuff, und die schräge 3D-Tastatur zeigt jede Taste: die nächste hüpft und leuchtet, eine richtige sinkt grün ein, eine falsche blinkt rot. Das Rennen lässt sich ausschalten.
- ⚛️ **Quantum Lab**: quantum physics for 11-year-olds, with few words and lots of cartoons. Professor Whiskers (Schrödinger's cat, with glasses) says one short line at a time, and every lab is an animated experiment with comic pop-ups (ZAP!, BONK!, POOF!), sounds and missions: 🪜 **Energy stairs** (zap Elly the electron with rainbow light; only the exact colours make her hop up), 🪙 **Quantum coin** (a 😺-and-🐟 qubit you spin, flip and look at; spin it twice and the 🐟 ways cancel, POOF), 🌊 **Shy electrons** (the double slit: stripes appear, until a big eye watches them), 👻 **Through walls** (balls BONK off the wall, the ghost electron sometimes tunnels through) and 🔗 **Spooky twins** (entangled twins and a real Bell test: no secret plan can match like they do). Two quick questions finish each lab, then a 10-question quiz.

Every Learning subject has the same three levels, ⏱️ Time Trial, 🏁 race against Robo, streaks and trophies. Word questions have answer buttons you can tap, or press the number keys.

## 🎮 Games

- 🏃 **Robot Tag**: a 3D-style parkour game. Run, double-jump and wall-jump to escape the robot taggers for 3 minutes. Another robot joins every minute, and they keep getting faster. The robots plan a real route to you, but they can't tag you through a wall or from under a platform. The runner has a face, a flapping headband and swinging arms, squashes when landing, and shouts "Double!" and "WALL JUMP!"; the robots have grumpy eyebrows, reach for you and chat ("BEEP BOOP!", "Come back!").
- 👻 **Pac-Man**: eat every dot and dodge the four ghosts. Gobble a big flashing dot and the ghosts turn blue, so you can chase them for bonus points. The maze is in 3D: raised walls, shiny dots and power pellets that float and glow, a Pac-Man with an eye and eyebrow, and ghosts that bob, shiver when scared and say cheeky things ("Boo!", "Nom nom?"), with "POWER UP!", "YUM!" and "OUCH!" pop-ups and sounds.
- 🟡 **Pac-Man 3D**: the same maze, ghosts and dots, but you play from inside Pac-Man's own eyes, down in the corridors. ← → turn at the next corner, ↑ goes ahead and ↓ turns around. A little map in the corner shows where everyone is. Eat a big dot, then chase the blue ghosts!
- 🧟 **Zombie Blaster**: a 3D-style shooter in a moonlit graveyard. Point the mouse and click to blast goo. Waves get bigger, with speedy runners from wave 2 and big brutes from wave 3. Clear a wave to win back a heart. Zombies mumble ("Braaains!", "Hug me!"), see stars when hit and go "SPLAT!" or "GOOPED!", with sounds.
- 🧗 **Parkour Run**: a 3D obby high in the sky. Jump across orange, yellow, blue, green and black blocks that climb up and drop down: rainbow steps, a kill brick run, skinny beams, a spiral tower and a big drop. Touch a red kill brick or fall off and you go back to your last 🚩 checkpoint. Reach the 🏆 as fast as you can! The runner wears a backwards cap and a backpack, throws their hands up when jumping, squashes on landing, screams "AAAAH!" on long falls and says funny things when they fall off ("Whoopsie!", "SPLAT!").

- ⛏️ **Block World**: a Minecraft-style sandbox that looks like the real thing. Walk, jump, 🏊 swim and 🕊️ fly around an island of hills, beaches, lakes and trees, with flowers, tall grass, drifting clouds and a square sun. Dig blocks out with a left click (bits fly off in the block's colours) and build with a right click. Pick from 11 blocks (grass, dirt, cobblestone, planks, log, leaves, sand, brick, glass, 🔦 torches and glowing glowstone) in the bar at the bottom, or with the number keys (0 and – for the last two). **Torches** stand on top of a block or hang on its side, lean out from the wall, have little flames rising off them, and light up what's around them; take away the block a torch is stuck to and it falls off. There is a 🌅 **day and night cycle** (a whole day takes 6 minutes): the sun and moon cross the sky, sunsets turn it orange, stars come out at night, and the world goes dark and blue, so torches and glowstone light up what's around them. Press T, or tap the clock in the top bar, to jump to another time of day. Blocks have crisp 16 × 16 textures, soft shadows in the corners, see-through leaves and glass, an outline round the block you point at, and the block you hold in your hand. It's drawn with WebGL at the screen's full sharpness, with no 3D library (a plainer ray-casting picture takes over on a device without WebGL). There are two modes, picked in the panel: **Creative** (every block, unlimited, and flying) and 🎒 **Survival**, like the real game. In Survival you dig blocks into a 36-slot inventory (open it with E or the 🎒 button), building uses them up, and digging takes time (shown by cracks) unless you have the right tool. Punch a tree for logs, turn them into planks and sticks in the 2 × 2 grid, make a **crafting table** for the 3 × 3 grid, then wooden and stone pickaxes, axes and shovels, torches (coal over a stick) and a **furnace** (8 cobblestone) that smelts sand into glass, cobblestone into stone and clay into bricks, burning coal, logs or planks. Coal ore hides in the hills and clay lies on the lake beds. A recipe book shows every recipe and fills the grid for you when you have the items. Your builds, inventory and furnaces are saved, and you can start a 🌱 new world any time. Press Esc (or P) to pause and get your mouse back. 🐷 Pigs and 🐔 chickens wander the hills, hop up steps, oink and cluck; poke one and it jumps and runs off squealing (chickens flap down from ledges).

- 🏎️ **Turbo Track**: a toy-car race seen from above, on an orange toy track floating high in the sky, with clouds, floating islands, hot-air balloons and birds far below. Pick your car (Muscle, Formula, Shark, Monster or Bubble) and its colour, even 🌈 rainbow or gold, then race three robot cars (🐢 Easy, 🚗 Medium or 🚀 Hard) for 3 laps against the clock. Hit the ⚡ speed boosters, fly off the ramp over the gap, go round the 🌀 loop-the-loop, and brake for the sharp turns. Too slow for the jump or the loop and you have to go back and try again! Every race is on a random track out of five: ☁️ Sky Loop, 🏜️ Cloud Canyon, 🌈 Rainbow Hairpins, ⛰️ Zigzag Mountain and 🏝️ Island Hopper. A 🎥 3D chase camera (the default) follows behind your car like a real racing game, with the track curving off to the horizon, floating islands and speed lines; the button switches back to the view from above. Pop-ups for "ZOOM!", "AIR TIME!", "PASSED! 😎", "BONK!" and more, with sounds.

- 🎣 **Fishing Beach**: stand on the beach and fish in a wide ocean, seen from the side with the sea cut open (the camera follows your float far out to sea): the sandy Shallows, a Coral Reef, the Deep Sea with a shipwreck, and the dark Abyss. One button does it all: hold to power up your cast, press when the float dips, then hold to keep the green bar on the fish. There are **500 different fish** (25 kinds × 20 versions, from Common to Mythic, some only at night) to collect in the 📖 Fish Book, and **8 rods** to unlock by catching special fish. Watch out: sometimes a bigger fish swims in and eats the fish on your hook, and then you have to reel in the big one! Day turns to sunset and a starry night every 6 minutes. Casts go "Whoosh!", the float goes "Plop!", and a hooked fish talks back ("Catch me!", "Not today!"), with sounds.
- 🏓 **Ping Pong 3D**: table tennis in 3D against Pong-Bot, seen from behind your end of the table in a sports hall with a cheering crowd. Your paddle follows the mouse (or your finger, or the arrow keys) and the ball bounces off it by itself: swing fast to hit harder, hit with the edge of the paddle to aim to the side, and swing down on a high ball to **smash** it. The ball has a shadow so you can tell how high it is, and on Easy and Medium a ring shows where it will bounce. Real rules: the serve bounces on your side first, two serves each, first to 11 wins by 2 clear points. Pong-Bot comes in 🐢 Easy, 🤖 Medium and 🔥 Hard, and the game remembers your longest rally. Pong-Bot taunts you ("Beep! Too easy!", "Error 404!") and the crowd goes "OOOOH!" at long rallies.
- 🥚 **Egg Heist**: a game like **Steal An Egg** (the most played game on Roblox right now) and Steal a Brainrot, in 3D with blocky Roblox-style players. Eggs ride along a conveyor belt (Common up to Mythic and SECRET, like the Rainbow, Cosmic and Banana Disco Eggs): walk up and press E to buy one, carry it home and it makes money every second, even more once it hatches into a pet. Walk over your pedestals to collect the cash. Then sneak into the three robot players' bases (BaconHair_77, xX_NoobSlayer_Xx and Guest_1337), grab their best egg and run home before they catch you! They'll come for your eggs too: lock your door for 30 seconds and bonk thieves with your bat to get your egg back. Sell eggs on the 💲 pad, and rebirth at $1M for a bigger income forever.
- 🧠 **Brainrot Heist**: the same game with the Italian brainrot characters, like **Steal a Brainrot** on Roblox (the all-time record holder, with 25 million players at once). From Noobini Pizzanini and Lirilì Larilà up to Mythic Bombardiro Crocodilo, Brainrot Gods Tralalero Tralala and Tung Tung Tung Sahur, and the SECRET La Vaca Saturno Saturnita and Strawberry Elephant, each drawn from bobbing emoji. Like in Steal a Brainrot, a brainrot you buy hops off the belt and walks home to your base by itself; stealing one, you still have to carry it and run!
- 🏗️ **Endless Tower**: a tower that never ends, like **Build a Tower** on Roblox. A block slides over the top of your tower: drop it at just the right moment! Whatever hangs over the edge gets chopped off, and PERFECT drops in a row make the tower wider again. Every block makes money each second (more for Gold, Diamond, Rainbow and Cosmic blocks from the crates, and more the higher you go): build past Cloud Land, into Space, the Moon, the Galaxy, the Void and beyond. A free wide floor every 25 blocks, money while you're away, rebirths, and a robot Auto builder after three rebirths.
- 🌱 **Garden Grow**: a game like **Grow a Garden** on Roblox (the fastest game ever to a billion visits). Buy seeds at Sam's shop (new stock every five minutes, rare seeds hardly ever), plant them, and they grow in real time, even while the game is closed. Carrots and pumpkins are picked once; strawberry bushes and apple, mango and Candy Blossom trees keep growing fruit. Every crop has its own weight (and some are HUGE), and the weather changes it: rain makes it Wet, snow makes it Chilled, both make it Frozen, and lightning makes it Shocked (×100!). Now and then one grows Gold or Rainbow. Water plants to grow them faster, and sell your basket at Steven's stand.
- 🌲 **99 Nights**: a game like **99 Nights in the Forest** on Roblox, in real 3D (WebGL). The campfire is the only safe place, and its flickering light really lights up the trees round it while fog and darkness close in everywhere else. Chop trees with your axe (they fall over: TIMBER!), carry the logs back in your sack and throw them on: the bigger the fire, the bigger the circle of light. Loot treasure chests (golden ones are rare!), find berries and carrots before you get hungry, sprint with Shift, and spend scrap at the crafting bench (a bigger sack, a better axe, a stronger flashlight, a stone fire pit). At night wolves with glowing eyes come out of the trees (every fifth night the huge Alpha Wolf), some nights are foggy, and the Deer, a tall shadow with red eyes and huge antlers, creeps up on anyone outside the light and then CHARGES while your heart pounds. Shine your flashlight right at it and it runs away! Bonk wolves for meat to cook on the fire, bring the four lost kids (Dino, Kraken, Squid and Koala Kid) home, and survive 99 nights to win.
- ⚽ **Blox Soccer**: 4 against 4 football in a stadium full of cheering fans, with the same blocky Roblox-style players as Tag Rush. You're on the blue team with Noob_Max, PixelMia and GK_Bob in goal, against the red team. Run into the ball to dribble, hold Space to shoot (the longer you hold, the harder the shot), press E to pass, and run into a red player with the ball to tackle them. The camera follows the ball and turns to face the goal when you have it. On phones and tablets you run with a 🕹️ joystick (push it a little to jog, all the way to sprint), with big PASS and SHOOT buttons right on the pitch. Keepers dive for shots, hard shots can be blocked or hit the post, and the fans jump up and down after every goal. Most goals in 3 minutes wins, and there are trophies for winning a match and for a hat trick.
- 🏀 **Blox Hoops**: 3 against 3 basketball on a wooden court, with the same blocky Roblox-style players. You're on the blue team with Noob_Max and PixelMia, against the red team. Run with the ball to dribble, hold Space and let go when the marker is in the green to shoot (the green part gets smaller further away), and press Space right under the hoop to **SLAM DUNK**! E passes, and a team-mate throws the ball in to you after every basket. Without the ball, Space jumps to block a shot, and running into the dribbler steals it. Shots from behind the big line are worth 3, and missed shots clank off the rim for a rebound. On phones and tablets you run with a 🕹️ joystick, with big PASS and SHOOT buttons on the court. Most points in 3 minutes wins, and there are trophies for a win, a dunk and a 3-pointer.

You and 4 bots (Noob_Max and friends) have to run from one safe zone to the other before the time runs out without getting tagged. Round 1 has one tagger, and every round adds another (up to 12). The farm has 10,000 potatoes: sell them all to finish the game.

Every game fills the screen and has touch buttons on phones and tablets.

The address bar keeps track of where you are: `#/` is home, `#/games` is a section, and `#/games/pacman` is an app. That makes the browser's back button work, and you can bookmark a game.

Progress is saved in this browser's `localStorage` under one key, shared by every app.

### Smoke test

Every pull request and every push to `main` runs a GitHub Actions check (`.github/workflows/smoke.yml`): it makes sure every script parses, then `tests/smoke.mjs` opens every Learning subject and every game in a headless Chromium, presses Play, and fails if any page throws an error. To run it yourself: `npm install --no-save playwright && npx playwright install chromium && node tests/smoke.mjs`.

### Adding a new app

1. Make a folder, like `learning/spelling/` or `games/snake/`, with a `.js` file (and a `.css` file if it needs one).
2. In the `.js` file, register the app:

   ```js
   (function(){
     "use strict";
     const store = Hub.store; // saved progress; call Hub.saveStore() after changing it

     Hub.register({
       id: "spelling", section: "learning",          // section is "learning" or "games"
       title: "Spelling Bee", icon: "🐝",
       blurb: "One line about the app, for its card.",
       stat: () => store.spellingBest ? "Best: " + store.spellingBest : "", // optional
       badges: [                                     // optional: trophies for the section's shelf
         { id: "spell10", icon: "🐝", name: "Busy Bee", desc: "Spell 10 words right", test: () => (store.spellingRight || 0) >= 10 }
       ],
       mount
     });

     // called the first time the app is opened: build it inside host
     function mount(host){
       host.innerHTML = `<div class="wrap"><a class="back-link" href="#/learning">← Learning</a> ...</div>`;
       return {
         open(){ /* shown: start timers or game loops */ },
         close(){ /* hidden: stop them */ }
       };
     }
   })();
   ```

3. Add its `<link>` and `<script>` lines to `index.html`, next to the other apps.

A new Learning subject is even simpler: it only brings its topics and calls `Notebook.register({...})` (see the top of `learning/notebook.js`), and it gets levels, worked answers, the Time Trial, the race and trophies for free.

The app shows up in its section's list, and its trophies go on that section's shelf. Call `Hub.checkBadges()` after progress changes to award new trophies. Games can reuse the `game-screen` frame from `games/game-frame.css`, and call `Hub.leave()` from their Exit button.
